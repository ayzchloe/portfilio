About

I'm Ayzm Abbas, a full-stack web developer and AI builder based in Pakistan. I run my own web & AI development agency, where I design and ship products end to end — from Laravel/PHP backends and Tailwind front ends to Python-based AI agents that handle real business workflows.

My work leans toward automation: multi-bot deployment platforms, SEO agents, and game-generation tools. I build these both as client deliverables on Upwork and as portfolio pieces for display, and I take part in hackathons along the way.

Alongside client work, I've contributed to group software projects and completed an internship coordinating a technology incubator's youth program — so I'm as comfortable managing people and delivery as I am writing code.
